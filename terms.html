2. The service

Doompush is an iOS app that lets you block apps of your choice and earn screen time by doing camera-verified exercise. The core loop — blocking, earning, your creature and its evolution — is free. "Doompush Premium" is an optional auto-renewing subscription that adds cosmetics, extra exercises, deeper statistics, step-based minutes and a monthly streak shield.

Doompush is rated 9+ on the App Store. Rewarded ads and usage statistics are only switched on for people whose age range, as shared through iOS, is 16 or over; for everyone else they are off, as the Privacy Policy explains. There are no accounts and no sign-in: everything is tied to your device and your Apple Account.
