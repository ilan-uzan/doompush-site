1. The service

Doompush is an iOS app that lets you block apps of your choice and earn screen time by doing camera-verified exercise. The core loop (blocking, earning, your creature and its evolution) is free. "Doompush Premium" is an optional auto-renewing subscription that adds cosmetics, extra exercises, deeper statistics and a monthly streak shield.
