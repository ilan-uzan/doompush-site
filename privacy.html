Privacy Policy

Doompush · Effective 8 September 2026

The short version. Your camera feed never leaves your phone. We do not have accounts, we do not sell data, and the only things that leave your device are anonymous usage signals, subscription receipts handled by Apple and RevenueCat, and, only if you choose to watch a rewarded ad, the ad request itself.

What Doompush does on your device

  • Camera. Reps are counted with Apple's on-device Vision framework. Frames are analysed in memory and discarded. No photo or video is stored, uploaded or shared. Doompush does not work without the camera because reps could not be verified.
  • Screen Time (Family Controls). With your permission, Doompush shields the apps you pick and meters your earned unlock windows. Apple's Screen Time API keeps app names and usage on the device; Doompush only ever sees opaque tokens and a "budget used up" signal from its own extension.
  • Apple Health. If you allow it, camera-verified sessions are written to Health as strength workouts. Doompush does not read Health data.
  • Notifications. Local reminders (creature hungry, streak at risk, stage warning, window closed) are scheduled on the device. You can turn each one off in You ▸ Notifications.
  • Your progress (balance, streak, creature stage, settings) is stored on your device and in your iCloud device backup. Deleting the app deletes it.

What leaves your device

  • Purchases. Subscriptions are processed by Apple. Receipts are validated by RevenueCat so your Premium status can be restored. We never see your payment details.
  • Anonymous analytics. We use TelemetryDeck to count events such as "session completed" or "paywall shown". Signals are anonymised before they leave the phone; no advertising identifier is used and the data cannot be linked to you.
  • Rewarded ads (free plan, optional). If you tap "Watch" on the bonus chest, an ad from Google AdMob is requested. Only then does iOS ask whether the ad may use tracking; if you decline, you still get a non-personalised ad. Premium users never see this. See Google's advertising policies.

Children

Doompush is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

  • Revoke camera, Screen Time, Health or notification access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Manage or cancel the subscription in Settings ▸ Apple Account ▸ Subscriptions.
  • Delete the app to erase all locally stored progress.

Changes and contact

We will update this page when the policy changes. Questions: support@doompush.app.
